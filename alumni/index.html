The EEA Alumni Network

A network in formation — and one we will build together.

An Alumni Network on Day One

EEA is six months old. Our first cohort will not graduate until 2029. And yet — somewhat unusually for a new institution — EEA already has an alumni network of more than 240 members.

This is because, in 2024, EEA's founders launched a parallel programme called the Founders' Alumni Programme — open to graduates of any Greek or diaspora engineering institution who actively supported the establishment of EEA, by donating, mentoring, lecturing, or serving on a working group. Members of this network — engineers from NTUA, AUTh, EMP, Demokritos, but also from Imperial, MIT, ETH and TU Delft — form the seed of what will become a much wider EEA alumni community in the years ahead.

What the Alumni Network Does

  • Co-ordinates the EEA mentorship programme, pairing alumni mentors with current undergraduates
  • Hosts the annual Alumni Symposium (next: 5–6 September 2026, on the Peania campus)
  • Manages the alumni fund, which supports student scholarships and the Daedalus Society
  • Maintains the EEA Engineering Speakers' Bureau — alumni who guest-lecture for EEA students
  • Operates regional chapters in Athens, Thessaloniki, London and New York

Join
