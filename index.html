A New Chapter in Greek Engineering

Where Innovation Meets a 3,000-Year Tradition of Knowledge

Established in November 2025 in the historic Peania district of Attica, Elliniki Engineering Academy is one of Greece's newest private institutions of higher education.

Though we welcomed our first cohort only six months ago, our 12,000 m² Peania campus, brand-new laboratories, and partnerships across the Athens technology corridor give our 286 founding students a head start few institutions can match.

  • EU-aligned curriculum benchmarked against TU Delft, ETH and NTUA standards
  • Bilingual instruction in Greek and English from day one
  • Student-to-faculty ratio of 8:1 — among the lowest in Greece
  • Located 25 minutes from Athens International Airport
